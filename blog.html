The Scent Glossary

Master the essential terminology of high perfumery.

Sillage

The trail left by a perfume when it's worn. It is the degree to which a fragrance's aroma can be perceived by others in the wake of the wearer.

Notes

Descriptors of scents that can be sensed upon the application of a perfume. They are divided into three classes: Top, Middle, and Base.

Dry Down

The final phase of a fragrance as the top and heart notes evaporate, leaving the heaviest, most persistent base notes to linger for hours.

Accord

A balanced blend of three or four notes which lose their individual identity to create a completely new, unified odor impression.
