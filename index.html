Fragrance Education

Understanding the Scent Pyramid

Fragrances evolve over time. Knowing the notes helps you appreciate the journey of a perfume from the first spray to the final dry down.

Top Notes

The first impression. Typically citrus or herbal, these notes are light and disappear within 15-30 minutes of application.

Heart Notes

The soul of the scent. Florals, spices, or fruits that emerge once the top notes fade, lasting for 2-4 hours.

Base Notes

The lasting foundation. Deep woods, musk, or resins that provide longevity and stay on your skin for 6-12+ hours.
