Our Mission

Democratizing Luxury Scent Discovery

Buying a RS 25,000 bottle of perfume blindly is a gamble. At LUXE, we believe that finding your signature scent shouldn't be a luxury reserved for a few, but a personal journey accessible to all.

We started LUXE because we were tired of "blind buying" expensive designer and niche fragrances, only to realize the scent didn't match our skin chemistry once we got home. Reading notes online isn't enough; perfume must be worn.

The 100% Authentic Guarantee

Trust is the foundation of our business. The perfume community is rightfully skeptical of fakes, dilutions, and scams. We completely eliminate this fear.

Every single decant we sell is hand-poured from original, verifiable manufacturer bottles. We purchase our stock from authorized retailers and direct brand boutiques. We never stretch, dilute, or alter the juice.

Our Sterile Clinical Process

We treat every drop of luxury fragrance with the utmost respect. Our extraction process guarantees purity.

No Air Exposure

We use sterile medical-grade syringes to extract the liquid directly from the atomizer tube of the original bottle. This means the juice never touches open air until you spray it, preserving top notes perfectly.

Premium Glass Atomizers

We do not use cheap plastic decants. Every sample is housed in thick, premium glass vials with high-quality sprayers that deliver a fine, even mist similar to the original bottles.

Teflon Sealed

To ensure zero leakage during transit, especially in Indian weather conditions, every threaded neck is wrapped with anti-leak teflon tape before being securely capped and packed.
